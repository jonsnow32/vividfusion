Privacy Policy for VividFusion

Last updated: 2026-06-03

VividFusion ("the app", "we", "us") is a media player for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how. By using VividFusion you agree to this policy.

Our position on content

VividFusion does not host, index, provide, or recommend any media content. The app only plays media files, stream links, magnet links, and .torrent files that you provide, and connects to third-party services using accounts and credentials that you supply. We do not collect, store, or transmit the content you choose to play.

Information we collect

We do not require an account to use VividFusion and we do not collect personally identifiable information ourselves. The app uses the following third-party services, which may process limited data:

1. Advertising — Google AdMob

We show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the device Advertising ID and related data to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

2. Crash & diagnostics — Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to collect anonymous crash reports and diagnostic information (e.g. device model, OS version, app state at crash time) to fix bugs and improve stability. See: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. Services you connect (optional)

If you choose to connect them, the app communicates with third-party services using credentials you provide:

  • Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize — to unrestrict links you provide. Credentials/tokens are stored locally on your device only.
  • OpenSubtitles — to search and download subtitles you request.

Your debrid and OpenSubtitles credentials are stored only on your device and are sent only to those respective services. We never receive them.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device (not transmitted to us): URL/playback history, player settings, download state, and playback progress. You can clear this by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet / Network state — stream and download media, serve ads.
  • Read media (video/audio) / storage — browse and play local media files.
  • Post notifications — show playback and download notifications.
  • Foreground service — background and media playback.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — used by AdMob for advertising.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell your personal data. Data processed by AdMob and Firebase is handled under Google's privacy terms linked above.

Children

VividFusion is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
