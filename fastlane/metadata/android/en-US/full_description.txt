VividFusion is a powerful, modern video player for Android. Play your own media files and the stream links you provide — direct URLs, HLS/DASH streams, magnet links and .torrent files — with a polished player built on Media3/ExoPlayer.

VividFusion does not host, index or provide any content. You bring your own links and accounts; the app simply plays them.

PLAYER

• Media3/ExoPlayer with FFmpeg decoders for a wide range of codecs
• HLS, DASH, SmoothStreaming and direct HTTP(S) links
• Picture-in-Picture and background playback with media controls
• Audio / video / subtitle track selection
• Subtitle styling and delay adjustment
• Android TV & Leanback support

DEBRID SUPPORT

Connect your own Real-Debrid, AllDebrid or Premiumize account to unrestrict the links you already have into fast, direct streams — entirely under your control. Accounts and links are provided by you.

TORRENT STREAMING

Open a magnet link or .torrent file you provide and stream it through the built-in torrent engine, without downloading the whole file first.

SUBTITLES

Search and load subtitles from OpenSubtitles in many languages, or load your own SRT/VTT/ASS/TTML files.

LOCAL FILES

Browse and play the video files already on your device.

PRIVACY

VividFusion does not collect the content you play. See our privacy policy for details on diagnostics and advertising.

Bring your own links and start playing in seconds.
